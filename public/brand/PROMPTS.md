# Üretim notları

Gerit logo paketi, yerleşik OpenAI ImageGen aracıyla iki aşamada üretildi.

## Ana işaret

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: master app icon and open-source brand mark for a keyboard-first workplace task manager
Primary request: Create an original, distinctive logo mark for "Gerit", whose name means to carry out or accomplish. Build a bold geometric implied letter G from an open task card and a forward-moving completion stroke, conveying focus, progress, and getting work done. It must not look like a generic checkbox.
Style/medium: premium modern vector-like logo mark; flat colors; minimal; crisp edges
Composition/framing: one centered square mark with a strong silhouette, balanced negative space, and generous transparent margin; readable at 24px and memorable at large size
Color palette: deep midnight navy, electric teal, and a very small warm amber accent; high contrast
Constraints: genuinely transparent background; no written text; no mockup; no gradient; no shadow; no 3D; no photorealism; no trademarked shapes; no watermark
```

## Küçük boyut varyantı

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit
Asset type: small-size app icon variant for favicon, navigation, and launcher
Input images: Image 1: the newly generated Gerit master brand mark
Primary request: Simplify Image 1 for excellent legibility at 24px while preserving its recognizable implied G/task-card silhouette, diagonal forward completion stroke, and small amber finish accent.
Style/medium: clean flat vector-like app icon
Composition/framing: center the single mark and make it fill about 78% of a square canvas with even transparent padding
Color palette: solid deep midnight navy, solid electric teal, tiny solid warm amber accent
Constraints: genuinely transparent background; preserve the core Gerit concept; use only flat solid fills; remove gradients, texture, glow, shadow, and fine decorative lines; no text; no mockup; no 3D; no watermark
```
